JOJODLE

Jojodle is the daily character guessing game inspired by Wordle. Test your knowledge of JOJO's BIZARRE ADVENTURE manga series and try to guess their characters in this fun challenge.

How to play jojodle?

  1. Jojodle General Information

    Every day, guess a different character from the manga/anime JOJO's BIZARRE ADVENTURE. JOJO's BIZARRE ADVENTURE is a registered trademark, who do not endorse nor sponsor this project. Challenge yourself in this fun and interactive daily character guessing game. Each day, a new character is waiting to be guessed in this wordle like quiz, and it's up to you to figure out who they are. Compete with friends, share your daily score or streak with your communities, and test your anime knowledge as you play. Can you guess the character before time runs out? Join the excitement and see how well you know the world of "JOJO's BIZARRE ADVENTURE". The Character changes every 24 hours based on your timezone as indicated below.

  2. Jojodle Game Explanation

    Simply type in the name of a character and it will reveal its properties. The color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the character to find. Green indicates the property is an exact match. Red indicates there is no overlap between your guess and the property. ▲ ▼ With arrows, it also indicates if the answer property is above or below your guess.

  3. Jojodle Hints

    To help you find the character, you get to unlock clues after several guesses. The clue can be a quote from the character or about the character or mainly their title or something they done. If you guessed the character, you can come see at the bottom the clues of that day's character.
